Brown Noise: Tinnitus & Sleep — privacy policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

This policy describes Brown Noise: Tinnitus & Sleep for Android, provided by Jon Jovinsson. Contact: jonjovinsson@gmail.com.

Summary

The app generates audio on your device. All nine built-in sounds, the full Editor, sleep timer, audio-output information and local Library are available without an account, a payment or an internet connection. The app has no sign-in, advertising, subscriptions or purchases.

The app does not send listening activity, sound settings or device identifiers to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Opening support or privacy links uses an external email or browser app; those services have their own data practices.

Data on your device

The app stores your saved sound names and settings, your latest sound and level, and preferences in its private storage. Output information is read from the phone's audio system to describe the connected output and test the rates it grants. It is not a hearing test and is not sent to the developer.

The app does not record audio or access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar or health records. It does not create a listening profile or store hearing measurements.

Device backup and deletion

The app allows Android to back up its local sound database, including locally saved sounds and the last sound settings. Other app preferences are excluded from the app's configured backup scope. Whether a backup or device transfer happens depends on Android and your device settings.

You can remove individual saved sounds in Library. To remove all local app data, use Android's app storage controls or uninstall the app. Device or cloud backup copies are managed through Android and your backup provider; they are not erased by the developer when you remove the app. Restoring a backup can restore saved sounds. The developer cannot retrieve your local Library for you.

If you contact support

If you choose to email jonjovinsson@gmail.com, the email service delivers your address and the information you include. The developer uses that correspondence to handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or medical records. Request access to, correction of or deletion of support correspondence through the same address. Support correspondence is kept while handling your request and for as long as needed to resolve related issues or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion at any time; any necessary retention will be explained in the response. Support correspondence is not used for advertising or sold.

Android, Google Play and external links

Android, your device manufacturer, Google Play, your backup provider and any email or browser app you choose may process information under their own settings and policies. Their system or store services are separate from this app's local audio processing. The absence of an in-app analytics or crash-reporting SDK does not disable operating-system diagnostics or Google Play's own services.

This website

This privacy and support page is hosted on GitHub Pages at jonjovinsson.com. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits without a GitHub account. These pages add no analytics, advertising, contact forms or cookies. The website is separate from the app's offline audio processing. See GitHub's privacy statement for the hosting provider's data practices.

Security and changes

Local data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app isolation and your device's security and encryption settings. No system is completely secure; use your device's screen lock and keep its software updated.

If the app's data practices change, the policy and effective date will be updated. Privacy questions and requests go to jonjovinsson@gmail.com.
