Brown Noise: Tinnitus & Sleep — local data and earlier test accounts

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Developer: Jon Jovinsson. Contact: jonjovinsson@gmail.com.

Current free offline app

The current free offline version has no sign-in, online account or Community service. You can delete individual saved sounds in Library. To remove all local app data, use Android's app storage controls or uninstall the app. Backups follow Android and your backup provider's settings and can restore saved sounds later.

There is no purchase to cancel and no subscription to end.

Accounts from earlier test versions

Earlier test versions allowed optional online Community accounts and publishing. Removing those features from the current app does not erase records already held by the earlier service. This page provides a separate support path for those records; it does not say that the current app creates or connects to an account.

Email jonjovinsson@gmail.com with the subject Earlier Tinnitus test account — deletion request. Include the email address you used for that test account and send the request from that address if possible. Do not send a password. The operator may need to verify ownership before acting. You do not need to install or sign in to the old app to make the request.

If you only browsed anonymously, explain that in the request. An abandoned anonymous identifier has no email address; the operator may be unable to identify the record from your email alone.

What an earlier-account request covers

Ask the operator to review the earlier sign-in account, public profile, published sounds, likes and any publication staging records associated with it. Reports may also contain the reporter's identifier and written reason; request their review or removal explicitly. If records must be retained for a legitimate reason, the response will explain which records, why, and for how long.

Other users' independent local copies cannot be removed remotely. Old used-sound markers contain a random identifier without the author's identity. Other users' likes and moderation reports may relate to removed sounds; they require separate review rather than a claim that deleting an authentication account erases them.

Deletion from the former service and its backups depends on the provider's processes. The developer will explain the scope and status when handling your request. Other users' independent copies and provider-controlled backup retention may limit what can be removed immediately.

Removing online features from this app did not itself delete earlier backend records. This page does not claim those records have already been erased.

For local data, support correspondence and website hosting, see the privacy policy.
