Brown Noise: Tinnitus & Sleep — local data and deletion

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Developer: Jon Jovinsson. Contact: jonjovinsson@gmail.com.

Local sounds and settings

The app works offline and has no online account. Your saved sounds and settings are stored on your device; the developer cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

You can delete individual saved sounds in Library. To remove all local app data, use Android's app storage controls to clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Device backups

Android may back up the local sound database, including saved sounds and the last sound settings, according to your device settings. Restoring a device backup can restore that data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not necessarily remove backup copies. Manage those through Android and your backup provider.

Support correspondence

If you have emailed support, you can request access to, correction of or deletion of that correspondence by emailing jonjovinsson@gmail.com. Any necessary retention will be explained in the response.

For details about local storage, support correspondence and website hosting, see the privacy policy.
